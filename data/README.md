# MotoFreeP synthetic data

Every number in these files is invented. They are generated by `src/generate_data.py` with a fixed random seed (2027), so running the script again produces identical files.

**Rep names** are borrowed from the 2026 MotoGP, Moto2 and MotoAmerica Superbike grids as a tribute to the sport. The riders have no connection to this project, and the sales figures attached to their names are fictional.

## Tables

| File | One row per | Rows | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `accounts.csv` | account, as of start of FY2027 | 1,500 | Territory potential; current ARR ($80M total) |
| `territories.csv` | territory | 44 (40 core + 4 overlay) | Hierarchy and whitespace potential by product |
| `reps.csv` | rep who held a seat FY2023-FY2026 | 58 | Tenure, ramp and attrition inputs for capacity |
| `history_core.csv` | core territory per fiscal year, FY2023-FY2026 | 160 | Quota and actuals; training data for the attainment regression |
| `history_overlay.csv` | overlay territory per fiscal year, FY2025-FY2026 | 8 | AI Race Coach quota and actuals |
| `opportunities.csv` | deal, FY2023-FY2026 plus open pipeline at 31 Dec 2026 | 12,506 | Sales cycle times, win rates, pipeline |
| `usage_monthly.csv` | account per month, Jan 2025-Dec 2026 | 11,280 | Telemetry credit burn-down and riders certified |

## Key fields

**accounts.csv**
- `simulator_stations`, `licensed_stations`: stations the account runs vs. stations licensed from MotoFreeP (the gap is Rider Seats whitespace)
- `telemetry_volume_index`: relative volume of session data the account generates
- `enrolled_students`: riders in training (academies and schools only)
- `owns_*`, `arr_*`: products owned today and their annual recurring revenue

**territories.csv**
- `overlay_territory_id`: which AI Race Coach overlay rep covers this core territory
- `potential_*_usd`: whitespace dollars still available to sell, by product

**reps.csv**
- `hire_date`, `departure_date`: blank departure means still in seat

**history_core.csv**
- `quota_*`, `actual_*`: dollars by product. Telemetry Cloud is credited 50% on committed contract value and 50% on consumed revenue (`actual_telemetry_credited_usd`)
- `attainment_pct`: credited actual / quota (1.00 = 100%)
- `rep_tenure_months_at_start`, `vacancy_months`, `potential_to_quota_ratio`, `pipeline_coverage_at_start`, `quota_growth_pct`, `telemetry_share_of_quota`: candidate regression predictors
- `enablement_hours`: a decoy field with **no** built-in effect on attainment

## How attainment was generated

Attainment comes from a hidden formula plus random noise (`ATTAINMENT_DRIVERS` in the script). Pipeline coverage helps, quota growth and a high Telemetry Cloud share hurt, and rep tenure lifts results on a curve that flattens after the first year. Because the relationships were planted, a regression on this data demonstrates the method; it does not discover a real-world truth.

**opportunities.csv** (generated by `src/generate_opportunities.py`)
- `stage`: Closed Won, Closed Lost, or Open (pipeline carried into FY2027)
- `amount_usd`: deal value. Closed Won Rider Seats and AI Race Coach deals add up exactly to the actuals in the history files. Telemetry Cloud deals are contract (committed) value, so they add up to `actual_telemetry_committed_usd`, not the 50/50 credited figure
- `cycle_days`: created to close, for closed deals
- `owner_territory_id`, `owner_rep_id`: who held the deal at close. AI Race Coach deals are owned by the overlay territory; the core territory is `territory_id`. A blank rep means the seat was vacant
- `close_date_or_expected`: actual close date, or expected close date for Open deals
