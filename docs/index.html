    How the numbers work. Team target = the leader's core number × over-assignment. Guardrail: no quota above × the rep's FY2027 capacity or more than + above their FY2026 rebased quota. The forecast is a regression fitted on FY2023–FY2026 history (tenure capped at 12 months, pipeline coverage, quota growth, potential ÷ quota, region and segment), chosen by chronological backtest. It's a gut check for prioritizing territories, not a commitment. New and heavily ramping territories get no forecast; use capacity instead.
